# Getirbakim design language

## Direction

A search-led auto-parts marketplace for individual vehicle owners, with a separate, denser workspace for professional services and administrators. Adapt Airbnb's clear discovery, rounded surfaces and restrained elevation to technical product information. Use Carbon's flat, structured approach for operational screens. Keep Getirbakim's identity and existing server-side business rules.

References: [Airbnb analysis](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/airbnb/DESIGN.md), [IBM analysis](https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md/blob/main/design-md/ibm/DESIGN.md), [Carbon data tables](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/core/components/data-table/guidelines). The collection contains third-party analyses; these are inspiration, not official brand specifications.

## Foundations

- Use local Inter at weights 400, 500 and 600. Keep all copy Turkish.
- Primary action: `#0077c7`; hover: `#0063b4`. Text: `#222831`; secondary text: `#626b75`; dividers: `#e5e7eb`; soft canvas: `#f7f8fa`; blue tint: `#eef7fd`.
- Reserve red for errors and warnings, not ordinary product prices. Distinguish stock/status with text as well as color.
- Error box: surface `--destructive-subtle` `#fff0ec` with text `--destructive-strong` `#a74130` (5.5:1, fine for 12–14px copy), applied through `storeErrorBox` or `bg-destructive-subtle text-destructive-strong`. Never use `bg-destructive/10` + `text-destructive` for body text (about 3.9–4.1:1). The hex pair is rejected by `test-store-button`; both tokens are storefront-only.
- Use a 4px spacing base, commonly 8/12/16/24/32/48/56px. Content caps at 1280px including 32px desktop or 20px mobile gutters.
- Storefront controls: 8px corners. Cards: 12–20px. Segmented search: pill on desktop, rounded stacked fields on mobile. Popovers, purchase summaries and the pinned phone header get restrained shadows; product cards primarily use borders.
- Administration: 0–4px corners, thin borders, flat surfaces, aligned tabular numbers. Scope its styles to `.admin-workspace` / supplier classes.

### Buttons and controls

Storefront buttons take their size and colour from `storeButton({intent,size})` (`src/components/store-button.ts`) on `ui/button`; `ui/button` itself stays the registry's.

- Sizes: hero 56px (`hero`, one per landing view; the home hero vehicle button is `secondary` / `hero`), primary 48px (`lg`: purchase and checkout actions), standard 44px (`md`), compact 40px (`compact`) only from md up in pointer toolbars, 44px on touch widths. Icon buttons are 44×44 (`icon`) whatever the glyph size. The header category strip (20/28px drawn) and the search submit (40×38 drawn) keep their drawn size from 601px up with a centred 44×44 `::before` hit area (`.tap-44` in `storefront.css`); new compact header controls reuse it. Keep at least 8px between adjacent buttons.
- Text: at least 14px; hero and primary 16px, primary weight 600. No 11–13px button labels.
- Colour roles: one filled primary per view (`--primary` `#0077c7`, hover `#0063b4`). Secondary is the blue tint (`--accent` `#eef7fd` with `--accent-foreground` `#0063b4` text) or an outline whose border (`--control-border` `#84909c`) is at least 3:1 on white. Red only for errors and destructive actions, green only for positive states.
- Focus: a 2px primary outline with a 2px offset on `storeButton` and the purchase form's `.buy-button` (white on dark photo backgrounds such as the newsletter band).
- Destructive confirmations (cancel order, delete address) are `danger`: a red tint with red text, never a filled red. A quiet destructive trigger is `ghost` with `text-muted-foreground hover:text-destructive`. Standalone text links are `link` / `md` with `px-0`; no `min-h-0`.
- Extras after the `storeButton(...)` call (the `className` of a `<Button>` and the same-file consts it uses) are layout only: width, flex, margin, position, gap, `px-0` / `px-2` on text links, `text-left` / `text-center`, `whitespace-normal`, `justify-between`, responsive `hidden`. `scripts/test-store-button.ts` rejects any `min-h-*`, `h-*`, `size-*`, `text-xs|sm|base|lg|[Npx]`, `bg-brand` or `border-brand` there (an `!` prefix included). Documented non-layout exceptions: the filter chip's `rounded-full` / `font-normal`, the vehicle-sheet option rows' `font-normal`, and the cookie bar "Tercihler" link's `underline`. Clickable storefront controls are `<Button>` + `storeButton`; a raw `<button>` is allowed only in the header (the category strip triggers, the search submit, the mega-menu and sheet category list rows, the sheet's on-photo back button) and the listed exceptions (kit package card, gallery thumbnail, card specs toggle, footer cookie link, garage trigger), enforced by a per-file count in the test. No hex colour on a button, trigger (`*Trigger`) or close tag. The card specs toggle is a 14px label with a 44px target at every width; the guard also covers `components/navigation`. In the header, the mega-menu and sheet controls are at least 44px with token colours: the mega close is `ghost` / `icon`, the sheet breadcrumb chips `outline` / `md`, the search "Temizle" `link` / `md` and "… için tüm sonuçlar" `secondary` / `md`. Dialog and sheet close buttons are `ghost` / `icon`. Selectable chips are `outline` with an `aria-pressed` accent tint, never a filled primary. Pagination: “Sonraki sayfa” is primary, “Önceki sayfa” is outline.
- “Sepete ekle” on the listing card and the product page (`.buy-button`) is the filled primary: 48px, 14px/600 on the card (44px on a phone grid card, wrapping to two lines below 360px rather than shrinking), 16px/600 on the product page. The add-to-cart dialog's suggestion “Sepete ekle” and “Alışverişe devam et” are outline (`md` / `lg`); its “Siparişi tamamla” is the dialog's one filled primary.

## Discovery and commerce

- Home first screen: a value-proposition H1 (no compatibility claim), a secondary vehicle button, a 3-item trust line sourced from the policy pages, static featured cards and no promo carousel.
- Make real product-name/brand and product/OEM-code search the main entry point. The search mode must travel with the query; category searches retain their category. On phones the header's mode selector is an icon control (its accessible name carries the selected mode) and still submits `searchBy`.
- Vehicle registration is a secondary entry. It saves a preference and does not filter the catalog: on a listing's first page the products linked to the garage vehicle come first, in an “… aracına uyanlar” row above the unchanged results, with a link to the fit filter. Never imply verified vehicle-part compatibility without supporting data.
- Default consumer catalogs to a card grid at every width (two columns on phones, 320px included); approved service accounts start in list view. Let both switch views. Show name, brand, product code, available OEM, price and stock clearly; progressively disclose long technical descriptions. A phone grid card keeps the stock line and a full-width “Sepete ekle”; its technical rows open behind a toggle.
- Desktop catalog: filter sidebar plus results. Mobile: filters in a bottom sheet (“Filtrele”) with the live result count, a clear action and removable chips; selected filters show as removable chips above the results at every width; sub-categories as one sideways-scrolling row of chips, a short hero (heading and garage picker; the header carries the code search), products before the introduction text, and 44px touch targets. Preserve URL-based filtering, sorting and pagination.
- Product detail: clear heading, gallery and technical attributes on the left; sticky purchase summary on the right. At 600px and below, the same quantity/purchase form becomes a fixed bottom action bar. Reserve space so it does not obscure the last page content.
- Supplier observations with unconfirmed retail price/stock retain their preparation state and have no purchase form. Do not add invented ratings, offers, delivery dates, photos or compatibility badges.
- A running campaign (#580) shows the regular price struck through (`<s>` with a screen-reader "Önceki fiyat", `text-muted-foreground`) beside the selling price, and its end date ("Kampanya … tarihine kadar", Istanbul time) where space allows; no percentage badges, countdowns or urgency copy. Cart and maintenance kits show the selling price only.

## Operational workspace and accessibility

- Use compact filter toolbars, legible table headers, alternating rows, restrained hover, right-aligned numbers and sticky headers within scrollable tables. Make horizontal table regions keyboard-focusable.
- Keep existing product, sync and transfer tabs, status messages and server actions. UI styling must not trigger supplier imports.
- Provide visible keyboard focus, semantic search regions and headings, labeled quantities, and native dialog focus/Escape behavior. Keep primary touch controls at least 44px tall.
- Support 320px, 390px, 768px and desktop layouts without document-level horizontal overflow. Respect reduced motion and mobile safe-area insets.

## Implementation map

`src/app/globals.css` owns the document reset, fonts and the shadcn tokens (storefront `:root`, admin `.admin-app`); `src/app/storefront.css` owns repeated storefront markup (header, purchase form, footer) and `src/app/catalog-card.css` the listing card; `src/components/store-button.ts` owns storefront button sizes and colours; `src/components/store-alert-classes.ts` owns the error box classes. Keep route styles scoped and verify production CSS order as well as development rendering. Feature components live under `components/auth`, `garage`, `navigation` and `privacy`; `components/ui` contains reusable controls and icons.

Shared chrome is component-based: `components/layout/site-header.tsx` (`<SiteHeader />`) and `service-strip.tsx` are rendered once by the `(store)` route group's layout (`app/(store)/layout.tsx`), so every storefront route gets the same header; `/yonetim` has its own shell: the workspace navigation (`components/admin/admin-nav.tsx`) in `app/yonetim/layout.tsx`. Build admin pages from `components/admin/ui` (`AdminPage`, `AdminPageHeader`, `AdminPanel`, `AdminColumns`, `StatGrid`/`Stat`, `StatusMessage`, `StatusBadge`, `EmptyState`, `TableRegion`) with `.admin-table`, `.admin-form` and `.admin-inline-form`, not with per-page headings, notices or panel markup. Every admin page and data loader keeps its own `requireAdmin()`: layouts do not re-run on client navigation. Keep header controls at least 44px in both dimensions; at 370px and below the wordmark contracts to make room. On phones (600px and below) every route, home included, uses one header layout: logo and tools, a full-width search, then the category strip; the utility band is hidden. Once scrolled past, the phone header hides on the way down and returns after ~24px upward scroll with only the logo/tools and search rows (no category strip); it stays shown while the search field is focused or a header menu is open, the sticky frame keeps its space in the flow so nothing shifts, and reduced motion drops the slide. Form fields stay at 16px on phones so iOS Safari does not zoom on focus; that one rule lives in `app/storefront.css`, scoped to the storefront so `/yonetim` keeps its sizes.

The Trodo measurement notes under `docs/design-reference` are historical asset/layout references, not the current layout specification. Existing local images and fonts remain usable.
